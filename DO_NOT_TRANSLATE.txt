# transliterated (Superset-u -> Superset-у).

# the human-reviewed upstream Serbian Cyrillic catalog keeps. Ordinary words
# (filter, table, data...) must never be listed here: tools/check.py will treat
# a translated DNT term as an error.
#
# Words the transliterator already keeps without being listed: code-like tokens
# copied from the msgid (ALL-CAPS, digits, underscores, camelCase), dotted names,
# paths, file extensions, and words with q/w/x/y.
